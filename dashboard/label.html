How: zoom is set for you. Judge the roof inside the orange outline only. Metal sheet: corrugated lines, often blue, green or silver. Concrete: flat grey or beige slab, AC units on top. Clay tile: red or brown tiles. Bitumen: very dark membrane. White / cool: bright white coating. Not sure? Press Unsure. Labels save automatically in this browser. When done, download the CSV. Accuracy check files go in validation/labels/, then run python validation/score_independent.py. Roof files go in qaydh_outputs/roof_labels/ (re-run notebook cell 10g-b). For the accuracy check, choose the class covering most of the 10 m square. The model's prediction is hidden on purpose (blind labelling).
